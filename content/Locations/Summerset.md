# Overview

[[Summerset]] is a bustling coastal town on the western shores, known for its thriving fishing industry and skilled harbor workers. Summerset serves as a major trading hub between inland settlements like [[Springdoor]] and the towns across the [[Breaking Sea]], with its lighthouse guiding ships safely to port through treacherous waters.

# Places of Interest

- **The Salt & Sage** - A cozy herbalist shop filled with drying herbs and bubbling potions, located near the harbor. The air is thick with the scent of sea salt and medicinal herbs, creating a unique coastal apothecary atmosphere.
- **The Fisherman's Rest** - A two-story timber-framed inn with a thatched roof, featuring a cozy common room with a large hearth where the smell of seafood stew and fresh-baked bread fills the air.
- **The Harbor Market** - A well-stocked general store carrying basic supplies, tools, and common goods, also buys and sells trade goods, fishing equipment, and maritime supplies.
- **The Docks** - A long wooden pier extending into the harbor, lined with fishing boats and merchant vessels, with nets hanging to dry and fishermen mending their gear.
- **The Lighthouse** - A tall stone tower standing on a rocky outcropping at the edge of the harbor, with a magical light that guides ships to safety for miles around.
- **The Old Church** - A weathered stone building with a tall spire, standing on a hill overlooking the town, now fallen into disuse but showing signs of its former grandeur.
- **Town Hall** - A stately two-story building of white stone and dark wood, with a clock tower that chimes on the hour and houses the town's government.

# People of Interest

- **NPC - Willow Goodbarrel** - Herbalist who runs The Salt & Sage and also owns The Harbor Market
- **NPC - Cork Shneebly** - Innkeeper of The Fisherman's Rest, known for his hospitality and local stories
- **NPC - Harbor Master** - Controls all shipping, trade, and harbor fees for the town
- **NPC - Klara Boneshine** - Keeper of The Lighthouse, maintains the magical light that guides ships
- **NPC - Griswold "The Pickaxe" Graves** - Caretaker of The Old Church, knows the town's history
- **NPC - Mayor Thaddeus Sterling** - Mayor who governs town affairs and public services from Town Hall

# Laws & Customs

- Harbor Master's Office controls all shipping, trade, and harbor fees
- Town Guard enforces curfew, handles local disputes, and maintains order
- Mayor's Office governs town affairs and public services
- Mandatory ship inspections for all vessels
- Harbor fees required for all vessels entering port
