# Overview

A quiet, unassuming village nestled along the trade roads between the bustling cities of [[Ekkrith]] and [[Galledrith]]. Ellrind serves as a simple rest stop for weary travelers and merchants, offering basic amenities without the complexity of larger settlements. The villagers are content with their peaceful way of life, where nothing exciting ever happens and that's exactly how they like it.

# Places of Interest

- **The Quiet Bloom** - A cozy herbalist shop with a well-tended herb garden, run by Mistress Willow. Offers simple remedies and common herbs.
- **The Crossroads Rest** - A two-story inn and tavern with a cozy common room and comfortable rooms. Famous for Bessie's rabbit stew and Tom's collection of traveler stories.
- **Thistle's Trading Post** - A modest general store carrying everything a traveler might need, from rope and lanterns to preserved foods and simple tools.
- **The Village Green** - Central gathering space with a large oak tree and stone well, where festivals are held and community decisions are made.

# People of Interest

- **Mistress Willow** - Gentle village herbalist who treats everything from scrapes to seasonal sniffles
- **Tom Goodheart** - Friendly innkeeper who knows every traveler's story and collects tales from distant lands
- **Bessie Copperpot** - Village cook famous for her hearty rabbit stew and simple but delicious meals
- **Old Man Thistle** - Meticulous storekeeper who records everything in his ledger and maintains maps of trade routes
- **Elder Rowan** - Village leader who presides over community meetings and keeps the peace

# Factions

- **Lake Merchants' Guild** - Maintains a small waystation for traveling merchants
- **Guild of Shadows** - No known presence (villagers are too simple for their schemes)
- **The Whispering Circle** - No influence (villagers prefer simple, honest magic)

# Laws & Customs

- No loud noises after sunset (except during the annual harvest festival)
- All travelers must register at the inn for safety records
- Mandatory sharing of news from other settlements
- No magic use without council approval (though this has never been needed)
- Village Council of three elected elders handles local disputes
- Road Warden maintains trade roads and helps lost travelers
