# Overview

[[Dimreed]] is a hardy, resourceful town that has adapted to its harsh surroundings with the [[Breaking Sea]] to its north and [[Illgadar Desert]] to its south. The buildings are constructed with sturdy stone and wood from the nearby mountains, designed to withstand both the scorching desert sun and the howling coastal winds. Life here is rugged, with a strong emphasis on community and mutual aid. The town's economy relies on a mix of fishing, mining in the [[Knifepoint Peaks]], and trade with passing merchants who brave the desert and sea routes.

# Places of Interest

- **[[Sirocco's Garden]]** - An apothecary carved partly into the rock cliffs, specializing in desert herbs and coastal remedies. The shop is decorated with sea-glass and desert sandstone, filled with bundles of dried herbs and clay jars of desert blossoms.
- **[[The Sand Sail Tavern]]** - A three-story inn built from salvaged ship hulls and desert stones, featuring a common room decorated with fishing nets and desert nomad tapestries. Offers comfortable lodging and hearty meals.
- **[[Knifepoint Provisions]]** - A well-stocked general store at the town crossroads, carrying everything travelers, miners, and fishers might need, from climbing gear to desert equipment.

# People of Interest

- **[[NPC - Jax "Driftwood" Barrow]]** - Innkeeper of [[The Sand Sail Tavern]], known for his hospitality and local knowledge
- **[[NPC - Hilda Ironstitch]]** - Merchant proprietor of [[Knifepoint Provisions]], maintains meticulous records of all transactions

# Laws & Customs

- Town Council elected by prominent merchants and guild leaders
- Harbor Master controls fishing rights and coastal trade
- Town Watch volunteers patrol streets and investigate disappearances
- Curfew enforced during cult activity
- Smuggling tolerated but taxed
- Desert travel requires registration
