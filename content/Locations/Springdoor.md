# Overview

[[Springdoor]] is a prosperous inland town that serves as a major commercial and administrative center. The town features a diverse array of shops and services, from magical item vendors to skilled craftspeople, making it a hub for adventurers and merchants alike. Springdoor's well-organized layout and professional atmosphere reflect its role as a regional center of commerce and governance.

# Places of Interest

- **Shrooms & Secrets** - A plaster-walled herbalist shop with a moss-green tile roof, filled with incense and alchemical vapor, specializing in potions and magical remedies.
- **One Eyed Basilisk** - A well-organized general store with a distinctive basilisk sign, offering a wide variety of goods and services for travelers and locals.
- **The Crooked Mattress** - A three-story timber-framed inn with a slightly tilted roof, known for its cozy common room and comfortable accommodations.
- **Temple of Pelor** - A pale sandstone temple that glows faintly at sunrise, featuring stained glass windows and a peaceful graveyard behind it.
- **To The Point** - A sturdy stone blacksmith shop with a large chimney, where the rhythmic sound of hammer on anvil can be heard from the street.
- **Dispel, That Ends Well** - A magical shop with a facade that shifts colors throughout the day, featuring floating shelves and animated displays.
- **The Watch** - A sturdy stone barracks with watchtowers at each corner, guarded by stone lions and housing the town's military forces.
- **The Town Hall** - A stately two-story building of white stone and dark wood, featuring a clock tower and serving as the center of local government.
- **The Cemetery** - A peaceful, well-maintained cemetery on the outskirts of town, shaded by ancient oak trees and tended with care.
