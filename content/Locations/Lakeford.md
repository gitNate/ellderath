# Overview

[[Lakeford]] is a thriving lakeside settlement nestled between the mineral-rich [[Mt. Vellan]] to the east and the timber resources of [[Brimbore]] to the west. Lakeford serves as a crucial trading hub, connecting mountain mining operations with coastal commerce across the [[Breaking Sea]] to [[Summerset]]. The town's economy thrives on both maritime trade and mountain resources, making it a prosperous center of commerce and industry.

# Places of Interest

- **The Mountain Bloom** - A sturdy stone herbalist shop with a herb garden that thrives in the mountain air, specializing in mountain herbs, healing potions, and remedies for mining injuries.
- **The Crossroads Inn** - A large three-story building constructed from both local stone and imported timber, featuring a massive hearth and displays mining tools alongside maritime artifacts.
- **Lakeford Trading Company** - A sprawling warehouse and store complex that handles both mountain minerals and maritime goods, serving as the central hub for Lakeford's diverse trade.
- **The Mountain Harbor** - A well-protected harbor built into the lakeshore, designed to handle both lake traffic and vessels crossing to [[Summerset]], featuring stone piers and cargo cranes.
- **The Miner's Guild Hall** - A large stone building that serves as the headquarters for mountain mining operations, containing meeting rooms and equipment storage.
- **The Crossroads Market** - An open-air market square where mountain goods, timber from [[Brimbore]], and coastal products from [[Summerset]] are traded daily.

# Factions

- Miner's Guild - Controls mountain trade and mining operations
- Merchant's Consortium - Oversees trade with [[Summerset]] and coastal settlements
- Harbor Master's Guild - Manages maritime operations and harbor security
- Crossroads Trading Company - Major trading organization with interests in both mountain and sea trade
