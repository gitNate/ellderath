Faction Type: Thieves Guild
Alignment: Lawful Neutral
Threat Level: Low
Known Territory: Throughout [[Ellderath]]

## Public Overview

The Guild of Shadows is widely recognized as the most powerful and organized thieves guild operating across [[Ellderath]]. While their exact headquarters location is unknown, they are believed to operate from a hidden base beneath the city of [[Springdoor]]. The guild maintains a strict hierarchy and is known for its professionalism and ability to operate with surprising impunity, suggesting deep political connections and careful management of their criminal enterprises.

Unlike many criminal organizations, the Guild of Shadows operates with a code of conduct that prevents them from being overly violent or disruptive. They are known to maintain a delicate balance between their criminal activities and the stability of the cities they operate in, making them a tolerated, presence in many areas.

## Known Leadership

The leader of the Guild of Shadows is known only as "The Shadow King," a title that has been passed down through generations. The true identity of the current Shadow King is a closely guarded secret, and it is said they wear a magical cloak known as the Mantle of Shadows that grants them extraordinary abilities. Some rumors suggest the position may be held by multiple individuals or that the current holder has maintained the position for an unusually long time.

The guild is organized into branches, each led by a Shadow Master. Known branch leaders include:
- "Silent Step" in [[Celladane]]
- "Whisper Wind" in [[Ruindrice]]
- "Ghost Hand" in [[Ekkrith]]
- "Silver Tongue" in [[Pertance]]

## Beliefs and Practices

The Guild of Shadows operates under what is commonly referred to as "The Shadow Code," though the exact details are known only to members. Publicly observable aspects include:
- They avoid unnecessary violence and killing
- They operate with a level of professionalism that suggests internal discipline

The guild is known to have a complex ranking system, though the exact structure is not public knowledge. They maintain safe houses and networks of contacts throughout their territories.

## Known Capabilities

The Guild of Shadows is known for their expertise in:

- **Theft and Burglary**: Professional-grade lockpicking, safe-cracking, and stealth operations
- **Information Gathering**: An extensive network of informants and spies
- **Fencing Operations**: Well-established networks for moving stolen goods
- **Territory Control**: They maintain control over criminal activities in their territories, preventing chaotic crime and maintaining order in the underworld

## Known Territories

The Guild of Shadows maintains a presence in several major cities across [[Ellderath]] making them a continent-wide organization.

## Public Symbols

The Guild of Shadows uses a system of symbols that can sometimes be found marked in public places, though their full meaning is known only to members. These symbols include:

- **Three Dots in a Triangle** (∴): Indicates guild presence or contact nearby
- **Twin Keys**: Two mirrored L-shapes forming an "X" - indicates a trusted fence nearby
- **Circle Within a Box**: A small circle inside a square - marks a safe house or trusted shelter
- **Hooked Arrow**: An arrow that turns into a hook - indicates a hidden exit or escape route
- **Open Hand**: A simple hand shape - marks locations with valuable targets
- **Broken Fang**: An open triangle with a jagged slash - warns of danger, guards, or traps
- **Hollow Square**: An empty square outline - marks locations with no value or that are abandoned

These symbols are typically found in inconspicuous locations and are often mistaken for simple graffiti by those not in the know.

## Public Warnings

**For Citizens**: The Guild of Shadows operates throughout [[Ellderath]]. While they are professional thieves, they are known to avoid unnecessary violence. However, crossing them or interfering with their operations can be dangerous. If you find yourself targeted by the guild, it's often safer to negotiate than to resist.

**For Authorities**: The Guild of Shadows maintains significant political connections and legal resources. Attempts to dismantle the organization have historically failed, often due to lack of evidence, witness intimidation, or legal maneuvering. They operate with enough subtlety and organization that direct confrontation is rarely effective.

**For Other Criminals**: The Guild of Shadows maintains strict territorial control. Operating in their territories without permission or attempting to steal from guild members is considered a serious offense and will be met with swift retribution.
